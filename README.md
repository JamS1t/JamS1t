# James Carl Sitsit

### I ship AI-powered web apps that actually make it to production.

I'm a full-stack developer based in Eastern Visayas, Philippines. I build AI-powered web apps, internal tools, and SaaS products, and I'm now taking on freelance projects for clients worldwide. I work async across US/EU hours. I care most about the part after the demo, when people depend on the software every day.

From 2022 to 2024 I worked at Litecloud PH on [CERPSYS](https://sitsit.dev/work/cerpsys/), a modular cloud ERP for Philippine small businesses, where I built the Vicinage subdivision module and most of the core: accounting, point of sale, inventory, and purchasing. Since 2024 I've been building and shipping my own products. TESDA CSS NC II certified.
